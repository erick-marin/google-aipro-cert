# AI for App Deployment

## What you'll learn

:heavy_check_mark: Work with AI to turn a rough idea into a plan that you can use to generate a working prototype.

:heavy_check_mark: Test your app, fix bugs with AI, and repeat to evolve your prototype into an app that works more reliably.

:heavy_check_mark: Turn your prototype into a shareable app by preparing it for first-time users, anchoring it in real documents, and managing access permissions.

:heavy_check_mark: Gain hands-on experience in AI tools like Google AI Studio to guide AI through the entire app development lifecycle.

## Skills you'll gain

- Artificial Intelligence
- Generative AI
- Application Development
- Debugging
